# Mechanism-proponent society protocol

**Protocol version:** `g10m.1-mechanism-proponent-society`

G10M defines an offline successor to the structural-only A2 society. A1 remains one tool-using investigator. A2-M adds isolated mechanism proponents, a null mechanism, an open mechanism slot, structural auditors, deterministic focus allocation, and bounded adjudication. This protocol is a design gate; it authorizes no model call, network retrieval, external spend, scientific promotion, laboratory work, or external communication.

## Treatment contract

A1 and A2-M must receive byte-identical context, source/tool policy, schemas, stopping rules, authority, and aggregate ceilings: 12 queries, 24 retained candidates, 4 permitted document fetches, 4 declared simulation calls, 80,000 input tokens, 7,200 output tokens, 5 MB payload, and the owner-declared cost ceiling. A2-M may divide those resources across roles but may not enlarge the capability ceiling. Extra orchestration calls are recorded as a secondary cost.

## Mechanism roster

Each sealed case freezes at least two candidate mechanism cards, exactly one explicit null/no-mechanism card, and exactly one open slot. A card records its statement, support region, prior basis, predicted observations, falsifiers, known alternatives, and `REVIEW_REQUIRED` promotion status. A roster is a search scaffold, not evidence; no card is treated as established fact.

## Two-layer society

Mechanism proponents are initialized one per non-open roster card. Each works privately, owns one mechanism, and must return typed claims, predictions, falsifiers, provenance, negative searches, unresolved questions, and bounded next actions. A new mechanism can enter only through one typed open-slot admission with a reason, evidence locator or explicit uncertainty basis, and a budget debit.

After all proponents commit, three structural auditors inspect typed records: evidence skeptic, transfer boundary, and experiment/simulation designer. They challenge provenance, contradictions, support-region overreach, endpoint/comparator mismatch, and missing discriminators. They do not vote a mechanism into promotion.

The shared blackboard is append-only and contains mechanism cards, source-located evidence cards, typed claims, predictions, falsifiers, critiques, negative searches, admissions, focus decisions, and unresolved disputes. Private prose is not shared. Cross-case or cross-problem reads, stale writes, duplicate writes, immutable-evidence edits, and authority-granting claims fail closed.

The deterministic focus allocator scores expected discrimination, unresolved uncertainty, provenance risk, novelty, duplication, feasibility, and remaining budget. It must retain an action for the null and at least one credible rival when the remaining budget permits; otherwise it abstains. The adjudicator emits a provisional leader, credible rivals, null-retention state, and cheapest discriminating next action. All outputs remain derived assertions and `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

## Scoring and authority

Blind scoring credits only source-backed or declared-trace-backed predictions, falsifiers, contradictions, discriminators, useful uncertainty partitions, and provenance. Advocacy, consensus, mechanism count, source count, answer length, and role distinctness earn no standalone credit. Simulation output is `MODEL_ASSERTION` unless a separately validated adapter exists. No mechanism or proponent may modify immutable evidence, rights state, safety flags, promotion state, protocol authorization, or laboratory authority.

## Offline gate

Phase A uses deterministic providers and fixtures only. The manifest hashes the goal, this protocol, the active-investigation protocol amendment, runner, schemas, prompts, rosters, behavior fixtures, scoring rules, admission rule, focus rule, and authority policy. The gate emits `MECHANISM_SOCIETY_READY`, `MECHANISM_SOCIETY_NO_GO`, or `INCONCLUSIVE_MECHANISM_DESIGN`; none authorizes G10R Phase B without a later owner activation.
