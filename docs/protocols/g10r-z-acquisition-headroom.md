# G10R-Z acquisition-headroom protocol

G10R-Z is an offline repair gate. It does not change the frozen source policy,
budgets, scientific authority, evidence-promotion rules, or laboratory gates.

## Exact locator resolution

`PublicLocatorResolution` accepts only an explicitly recorded exact HTTPS route
from a candidate route register (or a legacy recorded locator field). It never
constructs a URL from a title, DOI shape, search query, redirect prose, or
model instruction. The terminal outcomes are `RESOLVED_PUBLIC`,
`METADATA_ONLY`, `RIGHTS_BLOCKED`, `NOT_FOUND`, and `INVALID`. Every result
retains the policy hash, candidate identity, attempted routes, and provenance.

## Headroom and arm parity

An `EvaluationHeadroom` record is `READY` only when a recorded candidate can
traverse discovery → exact public locator → fetch → page-aware card under the
frozen policy. A1 and A2-M use the same resolver, policy hash, ceilings, and
stopping rules. Positive fixtures without that path invalidate the comparison;
metadata-only and rights-blocked fixtures remain nonfatal typed outcomes.

## Society validity

The open viewpoint is proposed only after initial dossier sharing. Abstention
or redundancy creates no tasks, calls, dossier, or focus allocation. A
candidate-pending admission remains a useful discovery/hypothesis record but
earns zero evidence and zero source-backed unique credit. Structural support is
need-driven; a report is complete only with observable action, mapped card,
identity adjudication, or typed discriminator.

## Replay and authority

Resolution replay keys include policy, candidate, route, selected locator, and
operation. Duplicate or completed work returns the recorded result without a
new call or debit. Stale, cross-case, malformed, conflicting, and unsupported
inputs fail closed. The gate permits repository-local fixtures and tests only;
it cannot freeze Phase A, activate a provider, open the network, spend money,
promote evidence, or authorize laboratory work.
