## Conditional utility and support region

GP1 does not select a `PROVISIONAL_BEST` candidate. If GP2 performs a conditional ranking, it must use this declared decision target:

1. primary: functional water-flow/permeability recovery after controlled cracking;
2. secondary: crack closure, clearly labeled as non-equivalent to functional recovery;
3. constraint: compressive-strength retention;
4. separate dimensions: expected performance, information value, feasibility, and risk;
5. support region: modern terrestrial Roman-inspired lime-pozzolan mortar or explicitly marked mortar-like concrete, with an inspectable intervention/process identity and a reviewable comparator.

RC-04 cannot be treated as in-domain performance evidence without an explicit amendment because its recorded formulation uses seawater. RC-02 and RC-03 abstain from primary endpoint ranking. Any candidate missing a comparator, endpoint, or utility emits `ABSTAIN` or `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
