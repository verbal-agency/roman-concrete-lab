# G10R active-investigation pilot report

## Outcome

`DO_NOT_ESCALATE_ACTIVE_SOCIETY`

The frozen four-case Phase-B comparison completed valid A1 and A2-M runs under
the same treatment budgets. The viewpoint society did not produce a qualifying
source-backed hypothesis or discriminator absent from the single investigator.
This is a decision not to escalate this society design, not a rejection of
active retrieval, open-world candidates, or agent societies in general.

## Budget and execution

- Sealed cases: 4
- Completed treatment/case runs: 8 (4 A1, 4 A2-M)
- Model: `gpt-6-luna`, reasoning effort `low`, `store=false`
- Aggregate recorded model spend: `$0.17469979` of the `$25.00` cap
- External retrieval: frozen exact-host policy only
- Simulations: unavailable adapters remained unavailable
- Replay: `REPLAYABLE_AFTER_REPAIR`, with zero critical failures

Every completed action is checkpointed. Historical checkpoints from the prior
manifest are preserved under `checkpoints/archive/` and their recorded spend
remains included in cumulative cap accounting.

## Boundary and next step

This result does not authorize laboratory work, evidence promotion, external
communication, unrestricted retrieval, or production adoption. The current
society design should not receive additional paid evaluation without a new,
separately frozen goal that explains how to improve its information acquisition
or tests a materially different topology. The A1 retrieval substrate and the
open-world candidate/evidence/authority plumbing remain reusable.
